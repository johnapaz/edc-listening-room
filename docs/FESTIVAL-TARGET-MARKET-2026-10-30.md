## Opportunity / recommendations

**Music-first discovery** (artist → vetted exact YouTube link → one real shared playlist) can later interoperate, with user consent, with a separately scoped **festival planner** (selected acts, conflict resolution, stages, approximate walking buffers, offline-capable maps, trustworthy schedule change reminders). This is a product hypothesis, not a patent claim. Large sites matter, but multi-venue events such as Reeperbahn Festival or SXSW can create comparable complexity.

**Early targets:** EDC Orlando 2026 field validation; Rolling Loud Florida, Forbidden Kingdom, Welcome to Rockville and Ultra Miami 2027 for regional follow-up. Compare EDC's EDM repertoire against Rockville's rock/metal, Rolling Loud's hip-hop and a Latin or Puerto Rican event to validate provider discovery across genres. The 2027 Rockville lineup is already announced (150+ acts over five stages), while Rolling Loud Florida 2027's lineup was not announced as of this research.

**Exclude inactive 2027 leads:** Bonnaroo explicitly announced a 2027 break; Okeechobee also announced a 2027 hiatus. Rocking the Daisies canceled its 2026 event, so it is not treated as a near-term confirmed opportunity. Do not claim either is upcoming.

## How ranking works

This is an **editorial, personalized commercial-target priority**, not an objective global attendance or festival-size league table. In order of importance: (a) timely event/lineup readiness, (b) music discoverability on YouTube Music, (c) affiliation with a reusable 20-company partner prospect, (d) festival routing/schedule complexity, (e) Winter Park/Orlando and Florida/Puerto Rico access, then other US/global reach, and (f) personal interest in dance/electronic, hip-hop, rock, Latin, jazz and discovering unfamiliar music.

**YouTube fit H/M is only a genre-based expectation, *not tested or verified* by querying the platform.** Even major musicians can have bad exact-track matches, licensing availability limits, mix-only recordings, alias/B2B ambiguity or music not available on YouTube Music. A real score must follow a representative lineup sample, verified exact video IDs and cross-account playback tests. Date symbols: **✓** checked forthcoming edition against accessible source; **~** recurring typical season, unannounced, reported or edition unverified. For all ~ dates, verify before contact; no automatic event recurrence assumption. Exact festival lineups, promoters and edition rights vary.

## Market and IP follow-up gates

1. Research first-party dates, lineups, published festival apps and current promoters for top ~15 before any external claim.
2. Sample a minimum of 20 artist/recording pairs per candidate against provider terms, test exact recording and YouTube Music access, then rank measured catalog coverage; source-backed artist identities, B2B mapping, rights, release freshness and no fake set times.
3. Document the technical differentiation, prior art and attorney-reviewed patent/trademark scope only if specific technical methods emerge; review event-name and data-redistribution licensing.
4. Interview a small set of fans, measure discovery conversion and return rates, and test one hypothetical organizer paid pilot; capture privacy, accessibility, low-signal reliability and non-intrusive reminders.
5. Only after MVP acceptance, owner approval and rights checks, propose a scoped organizer conversation or white-label prototype. No licensing outreach, public deployment, provider permissions, costs or companion integration authorized by research.
