How it works

Find it. Hear it.

Three steps from a name on the poster to music in your ears.

  1. 01

    Find an act

    Search every act on the lineup by name, including back-to-back (B2B) sets.

  2. 02

    Pick a day

    Filter to Friday, Saturday or Sunday to see who’s playing the day you’re going.

  3. 03

    Hear a song

    Tap a song to open that exact recording on YouTube. Playback happens there.

How songs are chosen

Checked by a person, not guessed by a search.

Every song link points to one specific recording that was reviewed for the right artist and the right version. We don’t send you to a YouTube search and hope for the best.

Some acts don’t have a song yet

Their page says so plainly, and they’re still listed with their day. More songs are added as they’re checked.

Set times only when confirmed

Day assignments come from the official lineup. Stages and set times appear only once they’re confirmed. The order acts are listed in is not the running order.

Questions

Good to know

Is this the official EDC app?

No. Listening Room is independent and unofficial, and isn’t affiliated with EDC or Insomniac. For tickets, hours, festival rules and the final schedule, use the official EDC Orlando site (opens in a new tab) ↗ or official app.

Do I need an account or to sign in?

No. There’s nothing to sign up for and no login. Open the link and browse.

Does music play inside Listening Room?

No. Songs open on YouTube (or the YouTube app, if you have it), and playback happens there under YouTube’s own terms. Some videos may be unavailable in certain countries or on some devices.

Why doesn’t my favorite artist have a song?

Songs are added only after someone confirms the exact recording, so coverage is still growing. Every act on the lineup is listed either way.

Is there a festival playlist?

When an event-wide playlist has been checked and is ready to share, you’ll find it on the Listen tab. Until then, the tab says it isn’t available rather than showing a link that doesn’t work.

Will it work at the festival?

It needs a data connection, and there’s no offline mode. Festival signal can be patchy, so it’s best for listening ahead of time.

What do you collect about me?

Nothing personal. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no ad trackers. When you open a song, YouTube’s privacy policy applies on YouTube.

I spotted a mistake. What should I do?

Wrong song, missing act or outdated day? Let the person who shared this link with you know, and it’ll be checked and fixed.
