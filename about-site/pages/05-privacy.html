What Listening Room collects

  • No accounts. There’s nothing to sign up for, so there’s no profile, email or password.
  • No cookies or tracking of our own. Listening Room doesn’t set cookies, run analytics or include ad trackers. YouTube can use cookies and other data if you choose Play all.
  • Nothing saved on your device. Your searches and filters live only in the page address, which is how Back and shared links work.

Hosting

Listening Room is a set of static pages hosted on Cloudflare. Like any website, the host handles your connection to deliver the pages, which involves your IP address and basic technical details such as your browser type.

YouTube artwork and tracks

Track artwork is loaded directly from YouTube’s image servers, so YouTube can see that your device requested those images. Listening Room asks your browser not to tell YouTube which page you were on.

Play all: Nothing from YouTube’s video player is loaded until you press Play all. Then a YouTube-hosted video player appears on this page and loads the reviewed videos as a queue. To make the video work, your browser sends YouTube the site origin and technical connection details; YouTube may use cookies, process playback data or require you to interact with its player. You can stop and close it at any time. The player is not a playlist published to a YouTube or YouTube Music account.

When you open an individual track, you leave Listening Room and go to YouTube, where {{ext https://policies.google.com/privacy | Google’s Privacy Policy}} and {{ext https://www.youtube.com/t/terms | YouTube’s Terms of Service}} apply. Listening Room never asks you to sign in to YouTube, Google or any other service, and never sees your YouTube account.

Spotify links

Listening Room loads nothing from Spotify: no player, scripts or images. Spotify only learns about you if you tap a Spotify button. Then you leave Listening Room for Spotify, where {{ext https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/ | Spotify’s Privacy Policy}} applies. Listening Room never connects to your Spotify account.

Accuracy

Listening Room is offered as is, for fun and discovery. The lineup, days, stages and set times are taken from official sources with care, but they can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Always confirm with the {{ext https://orlando.edc.com/ | official EDC Orlando site}} or official app before relying on anything here, especially set times.
