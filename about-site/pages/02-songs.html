One exact recording with checked sources

Before a track appears, we check:

  • It’s the right artist. Many artists share similar names, so identity is checked first.
  • It’s the right song. The YouTube video matches the released track.
  • It’s the right version, or the app says when it might not be (see below).
  • The link opens a specific video. Its current title and uploader agree with the source recording.

The original 12 tracks received personal playback review. Additional tracks have source-checked titles, uploaders, artist credits and artwork, with approval for inclusion. Those source checks do not claim personal playback. Availability can vary by country or account, and YouTube Music availability is not guaranteed.

About the artwork

Pictures next to tracks are the YouTube thumbnails for those exact videos, loaded directly from YouTube. They aren’t artist portraits, and Listening Room never uses another artist’s image or makes one up. When there’s no reviewed video, or a thumbnail can’t load, you’ll see the artist’s initials instead.
