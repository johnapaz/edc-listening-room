Stages and set times

Set times for {{stat poster_times}} entries were copied from the organizer’s daily set-time posters. They’re shown in Eastern Time and marked as provisional until they’re cross-checked against the official schedule. {{stat no_time}} entries don’t have a time yet, and say so.

A stage filter will appear on the lineup once stages are confirmed. Until then there isn’t one, so you won’t get a list that looks complete but isn’t.

Sorting by time isn’t a confirmed running order

Each day can be sorted {{ui By time}} or {{ui A–Z}}. By time orders acts by the start times on the organizer’s posters, with acts that have no time at the end. Those times haven’t been cross-checked, and acts on different stages overlap, so treat the order as a guide. To plan when to be where, check the official schedule.

When sources disagree

The lineup here follows the organizer’s current day lists, checked on {{stat checked_on}}. Older posters can show different billings. A day listing does not confirm a set time: times copied from posters remain marked as provisional. Check the official schedule before making plans.

Lineups change

Artists can be added, moved or cancelled close to the festival. Listening Room is updated when its sources are, but it can lag behind. Before you go, check the {{ext https://orlando.edc.com/ | official EDC Orlando site}} or official app for the final schedule, festival hours and any changes.
