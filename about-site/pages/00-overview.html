What it is

Festival lineups are long, and most names on the poster are new to someone. Listening Room turns the EDC Orlando 2026 lineup into something you can actually listen to: search for an act, see the day they play, and tap straight through to one specific recording of theirs on YouTube.

It’s an independent fan project. It isn’t run by the festival, the artists or YouTube.

What it isn’tNot the official EDC app, not a ticket site, and not a music player. Songs play on YouTube. For tickets, hours, festival rules and the final schedule, use the {{ext https://orlando.edc.com/ | official EDC Orlando site}} or official app.
