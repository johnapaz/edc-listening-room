The lineup

Listening Room opens on the lineup. Everything starts here.

{{shot lineup | The Listening Room lineup on a phone, with numbered markers on the search box, the day buttons, the With tracks filter, the Tracks to try cards and the first lineup row. | The lineup on a phone. Track artwork comes from YouTube; when it can’t load, you’ll see the artist’s initials instead.}}
  1. {{ui Search artists}} finds any act by name.
  2. Day buttons show everyone, or just Friday, Saturday or Sunday.
  3. {{ui With tracks}} hides acts that don’t have a track yet. {{ui All artists}} shows everyone again.
  4. {{ui Tracks to try}} highlights a few acts with a track, so there’s always something to play. All tracks → opens the full list.
  5. Lineup rows show each act, their day, how many tracks they have (“{{ui No tracks added}}” means none yet) and their set time when one is known.

With All days selected, the lineup is grouped under a heading for each day. Within a day, acts are listed alphabetically, which isn’t the running order; see {{page schedule#running-order | why}}.

Search for an act

Type any part of a name in the search box ({{ui Artist name or B2B partner}}). Results update as you type.

  • Accents and capitals don’t matter. {{app lineup?q=obskur | “obskur”}} finds {{hit obskur}}.
  • Partial names work. {{app lineup?q=garr | “garr”}} finds {{hit garr}}.
  • B2B partners are searchable. Searching either artist finds the shared set: {{app lineup?q=acraze | “acraze”}} finds ACRAZE B2B CID.
{{shot search | Search results for “acraze” showing the ACRAZE B2B CID set. | Searching one artist also finds their back-to-back sets.}}

No match? You’ll see “{{ui No artists match these filters.}}” with a {{ui Clear filters}} button that resets search, day and track filters.

Browse by day

Tap Fri, Sat or Sun to narrow the lineup. A short line above the list confirms how many acts you’re looking at. You can also tap the day shown under any act to see everyone playing that day.

{{list days}} {{shot day | The lineup filtered to Friday, Nov 6, with the Fri button selected. | Friday selected. The day you choose stays put when you open an act and come back.}}

Only acts with tracks

Most acts don’t have a reviewed track yet. Tap {{ui With tracks}} to see just the ones you can play now. It combines with search and days, and it’s part of the link if you share the view: {{app lineup?music=1 | acts with tracks}}.

{{shot tracks | The lineup with the With tracks filter selected, showing only acts that have tracks. | With tracks selected: only acts you can listen to right now.}}

Artist pages

Tap an act’s name to open its page. Here’s what you’ll find:

{{shot artist | Martin Garrix’s artist page with numbered markers on the set time line, the track title and the YouTube button. | An artist page with one track.}}
  1. Stage and time. Times are in Eastern Time. Times copied from an organizer poster are marked “{{ui not yet cross-checked}}”. Learn more in {{page schedule | Days, stages & set times}}.
  2. Track title. Opens that exact recording on YouTube.
  3. YouTube button. Does the same thing with a bigger target. A {{ui Spotify ↗}} button beside it, when there is one, opens the same song on Spotify.

Tap the artist’s name at the top to see every set they’re part of. Try {{app artist/edco26:2026-11-08:martin-garrix | Martin Garrix}}.

When an act doesn’t have a track yet

Every act on the lineup has a page, even before a track has been reviewed for them. You’ll see “{{ui No tracks have been added for this act.}}” instead of a guess, plus a link to the tracks that are available. Here’s {{app artist/edco26:2026-11-07:allnightkev | an example}}.

Back-to-back (B2B) sets

A B2B is two or more artists playing one set together. Listening Room keeps the billing exactly as announced and groups tracks under each artist, so you know whose track you’re hearing. Tap either name to see that artist on their own.

Opening a track

Track titles and YouTube buttons open the recording in a new tab, or in the YouTube app if you have it. Most tracks also have a Spotify button that opens the same song on Spotify, or in the Spotify app. Listening Room never plays music itself and never asks you to sign in to anything.

If a track says “{{ui Version may differ from the original release.}}”, it’s the right track by the right artist, but the video might be a different edit or length than the one you know. {{page songs | How tracks are chosen}} explains the review.

The Tracks tab

The bar at the bottom of the screen switches between {{ui Lineup}} and {{ui Tracks}}. Tracks gathers every reviewed track in one list, which is handy when you just want to press play.

Going back and sharing

  • Back works the way you expect. Your browser’s Back button, or {{ui ← Back to lineup}}, returns you to the same search, day and filter.
  • Every view has its own link. Copy the address from your browser to share an act, a day or a search. For example, here’s {{app lineup?day=2026-11-07 | Saturday’s lineup}}.
  • Keep it handy. On a phone, use your browser’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen to open Listening Room like an app.

On a tablet or computer

The same app spreads out on bigger screens, with more cards in Tracks to try and room for artwork. On a keyboard, press Tab to move between links and buttons, and Enter to open them. See {{page accessibility}} for more.
