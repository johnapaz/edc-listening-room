The lineup

Listening Room opens on the lineup. Everything starts here.

  1. Search finds any act by name.
  2. Day buttons show everyone, or just Friday, Saturday or Sunday.
  3. Discovery cards highlight a few acts that have an available track, so there’s always something to play.
  4. Lineup rows show each act, the day they play, and whether they have songs yet (“No tracks added” means there are no tracks for that act yet).

Acts appear day by day, in the order the official sources list them. That isn’t the running order; see {{page schedule#running-order | why}}.

Search for an act

Type any part of a name in Artist name or B2B partner Results update as you type.

  • Accents and capitals don’t matter. {{app lineup?q=obskur | “obskur”}} finds Obskür.
  • Partial names work. “garr” finds Martin Garrix.
  • B2B partners are searchable. Searching either artist finds the shared set. {{app lineup?q=luke%20dean | “luke dean”}} finds both of Luke Dean’s B2B sets.

No match? You’ll see “No artists match these filters.” Clear the search or choose All days.

Browse by day

Tap Fri, Sat or Sun to narrow the lineup. A short line above the list confirms how many acts you’re looking at. You can also tap the day shown under any act to see everyone playing that day.

Artist pages

Tap an act’s name to open its page. Here’s what you’ll find:

  1. Day, stage and time. Times are shown in Eastern Time. If they come from an organizer poster and haven’t been cross-checked, the page says so. Learn more in {{page schedule | Days, stages & set times}}.
  2. Song title. Opens that exact recording on YouTube.
  3. YouTube button. Does the same thing with a bigger target.

Tap the artist’s name at the top to see every set they’re part of. Try {{app artist/edco26:2026-11-08:martin-garrix | Martin Garrix}}.

When an act doesn’t have a song yet

Every act on the lineup has a page, even before a song has been reviewed for them. You’ll see “No tracks have been added for this act.” instead of a guess. Here’s {{app artist/edco26:2026-11-06:afrojack | an example}}.

Back-to-back (B2B) sets

A B2B is two or more artists playing one set together. Listening Room keeps the billing exactly as announced and groups songs under each artist, so you know whose track you’re hearing. Tap either name to see that artist on their own. To skip acts with no music links, select With tracks on the lineup.

Opening a song

Song titles and YouTube buttons open the recording in a new tab, or in the YouTube app if you have it. Listening Room never plays music itself and never asks you to sign in to anything.

If a song says Reviewed recording; version may differ, it’s the right song by the right artist, but it might be a different edit or length than you know. {{page songs | How songs are chosen}} explains the review.

The Tracks tab

The bar at the bottom of the screen switches between Lineup and Tracks. Tracks gathers every reviewed song in one list, which is handy when you just want to press play.

Going back and sharing

  • Back works the way you expect. Your browser’s Back button, or ← Back to lineup, returns you to the same search and day.
  • Every view has its own link. Copy the address from your browser to share an act, a day or a search. For example, here’s {{app lineup?day=2026-11-07 | Saturday’s lineup}}.
  • Keep it handy. On a phone, use your browser’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen to open Listening Room like an app.

On a tablet or computer

The same app spreads out on bigger screens, with more discovery cards and room for artwork. On a keyboard, press Tab to move between links and buttons, and Enter to open them. See {{page accessibility}} for more.
