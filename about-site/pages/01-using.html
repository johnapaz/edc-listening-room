The lineup

Listening Room opens on the lineup for one festival day. Before the festival that’s Friday; during it, it’s today.

{{shot lineup | The Listening Room lineup on a phone, with numbered markers on the day buttons, the Hear Friday cards, the sort switch and the first lineup row. | The lineup on a phone. Track artwork comes from YouTube; when it can’t load, you’ll see the artist’s initials instead.}}
  1. Day buttons switch between Friday, Saturday and Sunday, with the number of acts each day.
  2. Hear Friday (or Saturday, or Sunday) is a row of tracks from that day’s acts. Swipe it, or tap {{ui Show others}} to reorder the cards.
  3. {{ui By time}} and {{ui A–Z}} sort the day’s acts by poster start time or by name.
  4. Lineup rows show the start time, the act, their stage and how many tracks they have (“{{ui No tracks yet}}” means none). The round play button opens their first track on YouTube.

Times come from the organizer’s posters, so the line above the list says “{{ui Times are from organizer posters and can change.}}” Sorting by time isn’t a confirmed running order; see {{page schedule#running-order | why}}.

Search for an act

On a phone, tap the magnifying glass at the top. On a bigger screen the search box is always there ({{ui Artist or B2B partner}}). Search looks across all three days and updates as you type.

  • Accents and capitals don’t matter. {{app lineup?q=obskur | “obskur”}} finds {{hit obskur}}.
  • Partial names work. {{app lineup?q=garr | “garr”}} finds {{hit garr}}.
  • B2B partners are searchable. Searching either artist finds the shared set: {{app lineup?q=acraze | “acraze”}} finds ACRAZE B2B CID.
{{shot search | Search results for “acraze” showing the ACRAZE B2B CID set. | Searching one artist also finds their back-to-back sets.}}

No match? You’ll see a short message with a {{ui Clear search}} button. Tapping a day button also clears the search.

Browse by day

Tap Friday, Saturday or Sunday. The day you choose is part of the page’s link and stays put when you open an act and come back.

Artist pages

Tap an act’s name to open its page. Here’s what you’ll find:

{{shot artist | Martin Garrix’s artist page with numbered markers on the day, stage and time chips, the play button and the track list. | An artist page.}}
  1. Day, stage and time. Times are in Eastern Time. Times copied from an organizer poster are marked “{{ui poster time}}” in amber. Learn more in {{page schedule | Days, stages & set times}}.
  2. Play button. Opens the act’s first track on YouTube.
  3. Tracks. Each title, and the round button beside it, opens that exact recording on YouTube.

The share button at the top copies or shares a link to the act. The arrow at the top left goes back to the day you came from.

In a B2B, tap either artist’s name to see every set they’re part of. Try {{app artist/edco26:2026-11-08:martin-garrix | Martin Garrix}}.

Every act on the lineup has a page, even before a track has been reviewed for them. You’ll see “{{ui No tracks added yet for this act.}}” instead of a guess, plus a link to the tracks that are available. Here’s {{app artist/edco26:2026-11-07:allnightkev | an example}}.

Back-to-back (B2B) sets

A B2B is two or more artists playing one set together. Listening Room keeps the billing exactly as announced and groups tracks under each artist, so you know whose track you’re hearing. Tap either name to see that artist on their own.

Opening a track

Track titles and play buttons open the recording in a new tab, or in the YouTube app if you have it. Listening Room never plays music itself and never asks you to sign in to anything.

If a track says “{{ui Version may differ from the original release.}}”, it’s the right track by the right artist, but the video might be a different edit or length than the one you know. {{page songs | How tracks are chosen}} explains the review.

The Tracks tab

On a phone, the bar at the bottom of the screen switches between {{ui Lineup}} and {{ui Tracks}}. Tracks gathers every reviewed track in one list, with its own search ({{ui Track or artist}}) and day buttons.

Going back and sharing

  • Back works the way you expect. Your browser’s Back button, or the arrow at the top of an artist page, returns you to the same day, sort and search.
  • Every view has its own link. Copy the address from your browser to share an act, a day or a search. For example, here’s {{app lineup?day=2026-11-07 | Saturday’s lineup}}.
  • Keep it handy. On a phone, use your browser’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen to open Listening Room like an app.

On a tablet or computer

On a computer, Lineup and Tracks move to the top bar. On a wide screen, picking an act opens it in a panel beside the lineup, so you keep your place in the list; {{ui Open full page →}} shows it on its own. On a keyboard, press Tab to move between links and buttons, and Enter to open them. See {{page accessibility}} for more.
