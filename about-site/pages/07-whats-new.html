October 31, 2026: Listening Room is live

The first public version, built for EDC Orlando 2026.

  • The full lineup, {{stat lineup_entries}} entries across Friday to Sunday, with search that ignores accents and finds both artists in a B2B set.
  • Day filters, and day links on every act.
  • Artist pages with day, stage and poster set times, clearly marked when they haven’t been cross-checked.
  • {{stat songs}} reviewed songs from {{stat song_artists}} artists, each opening one exact recording on YouTube, with YouTube artwork.
  • Clickable artist names, B2B partners, song titles and days throughout.
  • A Tracks tab with every reviewed song in one place.
  • Links you can share for any act, day or search, and a Back button that keeps your place.
  • These About & Help pages, including {{page songs}}, {{page schedule}} and the {{page faq | FAQ}}.
