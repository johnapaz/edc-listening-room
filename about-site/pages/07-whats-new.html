October 31, 2026: Music-first redesign

  • The lineup opens on one day, with large Friday, Saturday and Sunday buttons and the act count for each.
  • A Hear Friday row (Saturday and Sunday too) puts that day’s tracks first.
  • Sort a day {{ui By time}} or {{ui A–Z}}. Times are still marked as coming from organizer posters.
  • Compact rows with a play button that opens the act’s first track on YouTube in one tap.
  • Artist pages lead with the artwork, day, stage and time, and one play button. Sharing an act is a button at the top.
  • On a computer: top navigation, and on wide screens a side panel that keeps your place in the lineup.
  • Search moves behind the magnifying glass on phones and looks across all three days. Lineup and Tracks searches no longer affect each other.

October 31, 2026: Spotify links

  • {{stat spotify_songs}} tracks now have a Spotify button next to YouTube, opening the same song on Spotify. {{page songs#spotify | How Spotify links are chosen}}.
  • A new {{ui Event playlists}} card links Insomniac’s official EDC Orlando 2026 playlist on Spotify, alongside our own YouTube Music playlist when it’s ready.

October 31, 2026: Current lineup and easier browsing

  • The lineup now matches the organizer’s current day lists: {{stat lineup_entries}} entries ({{stat day_friday}} Friday, {{stat day_saturday}} Saturday, {{stat day_sunday}} Sunday).
  • New With tracks filter showed only acts you could listen to. (Retired in the music-first update, now that nearly every act has a track.)
  • The lineup is grouped under day headings, and each row shows its set time, marked provisional when it comes from a poster.
  • Plainer wording throughout, and an About & help link at the top of the app.
  • Shared links keep the exact act or view, and show a preview card in messaging apps.
  • Track artwork fills its frame without black bars.

October 31, 2026: Listening Room launches

The first public version, built for EDC Orlando 2026.

  • The full lineup across Friday to Sunday, with search that ignores accents and finds both artists in a B2B set.
  • Day filters, and day links on every act.
  • Artist pages with day, stage and poster set times, clearly marked when they haven’t been cross-checked.
  • {{stat songs}} reviewed tracks from {{stat song_artists}} artists, each opening one exact recording on YouTube, with YouTube artwork.
  • Clickable artist names, B2B partners, track titles and days throughout.
  • A Tracks tab with every reviewed track in one place.
  • Links you can share for any act, day or search, and a Back button that keeps your place.
  • These About & Help pages, including {{page songs}}, {{page schedule}} and the {{page faq | FAQ}}.
